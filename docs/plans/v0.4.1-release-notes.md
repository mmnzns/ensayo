**What's new in 0.4.1**

**Your own key**
- Every provider's model list and prices were checked against the provider's own pages (2026-11-16). Ensayo now recommends each provider's best balance of judgment and cost, not its cheapest model. Your spending limit still warns before you go over.
- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** is new and is the Gemini default. Gemini models now think a little before scoring and more before longer reads, which keeps cost and waiting down.
- **OpenAI:** GPT-6.1 Sol is the default for scoring and reading, and GPT-6.1 Terra is new. Prices are now the published ones; GPT-6 Astra was counted far too low before.
- **Claude:** Fable 5.2 replaces Fable 5.1. **Mistral:** Mistral Large is the default (Medium is being retired). **OpenRouter:** Fable 5.2, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Mistral Large 4 replace older versions. Groq prices corrected.

**Built-in local AI**
- When no model is chosen and the recommended one isn't on your computer, Ensayo uses a model that is (for example Granite or Gemma), instead of saying none is downloaded.
- In setup, reading your first story with no model on the computer now says so plainly and takes you back to the AI step. The AI step also says when no model is on the computer yet.
