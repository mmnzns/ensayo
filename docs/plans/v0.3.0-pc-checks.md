# PC checks for Ensayo 0.3.0

For Miguel on the PC (RTX 5070 Ti, Ryzen 7 9800X3D), on the test build from Actions (artifact `ensayo-Windows`). These replace `engine-v0.2.2-windows-checks.md`.

Report each as pass or fail, with what you saw. If the engine is at fault, note it on "Voice Apps Engine Sync" › Still open. Don't run Delete all data on real data. Use a test Windows account for check 3.

## Start and update

1. **Install over 0.2.0 while Ensayo is scoring.**
   - Start an answer and stop it, then run the installer while "Listening back" shows.
   - The installer should wait or say Ensayo is busy.
   - Afterwards the answer is saved, your stories are all there, and your saved key still works.
2. **Start right after updating.** Watch the first 30 seconds. The window must never show "Not responding".
3. **Uninstall with the data box ticked** (test account only). Install 0.3.0 fresh, add one story, then uninstall and tick "Delete the application data". `Documents\Ensayo` must still hold the story.

## Microphone

4. **Headset.**
   - Plug in a headset, choose it in Settings › Speech › Microphone, then record.
   - The recording screen must name the headset, and the transcript must be clean.
5. **Muted in Windows.**
   - Turn off microphone access in Windows Settings, then press Record.
   - You should get a plain message naming Windows' microphone setting, not a code.
6. **Unplugged mid-answer.**
   - Unplug the microphone while recording.
   - The screen should stop with "The microphone went away".
   - "Score what I said" should work when you'd spoken more than 3 seconds.
7. **Pause.** Pause for 20 seconds, then resume. The clock skips the pause, and the 3:30 warning comes 3:30 of speaking later.

## Practice

8. **A full Drill with local AI** (Qwen 3.5 4B).
   - Progress rows tick as each step finishes, and the elapsed time counts.
   - Press Stop during one answer: the result keeps the measured checks and offers "Score with AI".
9. **A full Drill with your Claude key.**
   - Every answer gets a coach note, with no "couldn't read the reply" and no "too long".
   - Details shows "Judged by Claude …", and the session results show the cost.
10. **Spending cap pop-up.**
    - Set the cap to 25 cents and practise until the pop-up shows.
    - Check the amount, then press Keep going. The refused answer is scored again.
    - Keep practising until it shows again.
11. **A 2-second factual answer.** "When could you start?" answered with "In two weeks." It must be scored, not "Didn't catch that".
12. **Fix a word.** Mis-say a number on purpose, fix it with "Fix a word", and check "Said your number" changes.
13. **Quit mid-recording.**
    - Quit Ensayo while recording, start it again, and wait 10 minutes.
    - `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.craftconceptsdigital.ensayo\audio` must hold no loose `rec-*.wav`.
    - Home offers "Continue where you left off".

## Models and data

14. **Remove from this computer with Talaan open.**
    - With Talaan open and using the same model, it must say another app is using it.
    - With Talaan closed, it must remove the model and free the space.
15. **Delete all recordings.** Settings › Privacy. The audio folder is empty afterwards. Your stories and sessions stay.
16. **Move data to Documents.** The Home card "Move your data to Documents › Ensayo" moves everything. Ensayo opens the new folder, and the old copy is kept, renamed.
17. **Interviewer voice and headphones.** Unplug the headphones while the interviewer is speaking. The app must not hang; the next question works.
18. **Windows look.** Dark title bar, scrollbars, the window at 1366x768, and Windows text size at 125%: nothing cut off.
