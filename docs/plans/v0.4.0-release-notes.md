**What's new in 0.4.0**

**Local AI**
- **Gemma 4 E4B** from Google is a new choice for the built-in local AI (Settings, AI). It is a 5.2 GB download and needs 16 GB of memory. On a computer with 8 GB it shows greyed out and Ensayo keeps using Qwen3.5 4B, which stays the recommended model.

**Speech**
- **Parakeet Unified (25 languages)** from NVIDIA is a new speech choice (Settings, Speech, Show all models). It keeps punctuation and covers 25 European languages.
- Cohere Transcribe is not offered. Ensayo needs a time for every word, and that model gives text only.

**Under the hood**
- Ensayo now runs on voice-engine v0.4.0. The built-in local AI helper moved to the same version, which Gemma 4 needs.
