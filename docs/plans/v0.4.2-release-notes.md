**What's new in 0.4.2**

**Dates are right again**
- Ensayo had been using a sample date (October 21) for "Today", "In 3 days", the greeting, This week and which interviews are done. It now uses today's date.
- A few screens could show sample text in your app (a sample story on Add a story, sample numbers on Didn't catch that, and sample settings on Privacy while it loads). They now show only your own data.

**Stories**
- Title, company and year can be changed on the story page, and are saved with Save changes. Angles, practice history and applications stay linked.
- Add a story has an optional Title next to Company. When you fill it in, the AI read keeps it.
- Every number can be edited in place once saved, and "31% → 44%" stays a before-and-after.
- Numbers no longer show a doubled percent sign ("about 40%%").
- "What you owned" is a bigger text box on the review step.

**Applications**
- Target is a dropdown with each target's colour, and New target at the bottom makes one without leaving the form.
- Recheck (↻) sits beside the status pill on every tab, and on the overview. The overview says when stories changed since the posting was read, and you can choose to keep your edits or use the new reading.
- Interview stages fit their card: date, time and remove sit on their own line. A second stage of the same kind is named "Technical 2".
- A long resume file name no longer pushes Replace out of the card.

**Everywhere**
- A clearer "← Back to …" link at the top of each page.
- Long names and titles stay inside their cards down to the smallest window.
- Settings › Voice lists English voices first, with Premium and Enhanced at the top, and shows a newly downloaded voice without restarting.
