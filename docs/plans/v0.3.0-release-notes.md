**What's new in 0.3.0**

**AI feedback you can trust**
- Claude gets room to think, so replies no longer run out before they finish. A reply that is cut off is asked for again with more room.
- Claude, OpenAI and Gemini are held to the reply's format while they write. If they refuse, Ensayo falls back to the old way.
- Quotes shown as your words are checked against what you actually said.
- Each answer shows which model judged it.
- New question type for questions about yourself ("What's your greatest weakness?", "Builder or operator?"). These are judged on answering the literal question with one example, not on your pitch.
- A gap question you can answer "yes" to passes when you say yes and give proof.

**Your money**
- When a session reaches your spending limit, Ensayo shows what it has cost and asks whether to keep going.
- A model with no listed price is estimated high instead of counted as free.
- Every session screen shows what it cost.
- Repeat calls to Claude are cheaper (prompt caching).

**Practice**
- **Stop the built-in AI:** a Stop button wherever it is working. It also shows real progress and the time so far.
- **Your microphone:**
  - Choose it in Settings › Speech. Ensayo uses the one you chose and shows its name while you record.
  - Pause while recording, with a warning 30 seconds before the 4 minute limit.
  - If the microphone is unplugged, Ensayo says so and keeps what you said.
- **Fix a word:** correct a mis-heard word in the transcript and score the answer again.
- **If something fails:** Try again scores the same recording, so you don't have to record it again.
- **Short answers:** a short answer to a factual question ("In two weeks.") is accepted.
- **Practice this story and Rehearse** start a real session.
- **Esc:** it asks before throwing an answer away.
- **Continue after quitting:** "Continue where you left off" picks up a session.
- **Search** in stories, applications and sessions.

**Questions**
- New GTM and revenue operations pack (70 questions).
- 12 new general questions, including "What do you know about us?" and "How are you using AI in your work today?".
- Three more tool questions for lifecycle roles: Klaviyo, Customer.io, a data warehouse.

**Your data**
- New installs keep data in Documents › Ensayo. If yours is in Ensayo's app folder, Home offers to move it in one click.
- Deleted stories and applications can be restored for 7 days.
- Ensayo warns before you leave unsaved edits.
- **Safety fixes:**
  - Left-over recordings are cleaned up.
  - A damaged settings file is repaired or asked about.
  - One damaged file no longer stops the app.
  - Two computers sharing a folder are safer.
  - An update waits while an answer is being saved.
- Delete all data also removes backup copies, and says if anything couldn't be removed.
- "Remove from this computer" frees a model's space for every app (Talaan and Parlato too).

**Windows**
- The window no longer freezes at start.
- The interviewer's voice no longer hangs when headphones are unplugged.
- Plain microphone errors that say which Windows setting to change.
- Whisper models say they're slow on a Windows computer (Parakeet stays the default).

**Not checked yet**
- **Ollama and LM Studio context sizes:** not tested with a real server.
- **Built-in local AI scoring:** not yet compared with Claude on labelled answers.
- **A graphics-card default by card memory:** waits on the shared engine.
