**What's new in 0.5.0**

**A real coach after every answer**
- With your own key and a strong model (marked "Full coach" in Settings › AI, for example Claude Sonnet 5.5 or GPT-6.1 Sol), each answer now gets coached the way a person would: a verdict first (Ready, Nearly or Not yet), what worked in your own words, the one thing to work on next and why it matters to this interviewer, and up to three smaller things folded under More.
- The coach checks that you answered every part of the question. "Walk me through your background and why a consultancy" has two parts, and it tells you which one is still open.
- It coaches what you said, not your story bank. A number from a story is suggested once at most, not on every answer.
- Since your last try: on a second or third try it says what got better, what is still there, and what you lost. If the same fix keeps getting missed, it changes approach and asks you the question straight.
- Ready means move on: the answer gets "This one is ready. Move on." and Next question comes first.
- Session results show each question's path, for example Not yet → Nearly → Ready.
- It never makes up your experience: quotes are checked against what you said, and any line to say instead only uses your words and your stories.
- Smaller models and the AI on this computer keep the basic feedback, and say so.

**Stories**
- Add many at once: paste several stories, or pick files, a folder or a Notion export (Markdown, or the .zip). Ensayo splits them, reads each one, flags likely duplicates, and lets you review and save the ones you want. Nothing is saved until you choose.
- Story cards label a before-and-after headline "Result", so it no longer looks like a warning.

**Applications**
- "Stories to add for this job" on the overview: everything the posting asks for that none of your stories covers, with what the story should prove and a question it would answer. "Add this story" opens Add a story with the company filled in, and after saving offers a Recheck.

**Fixes**
- Settings › AI remembers your local AI and model when you switch to your own key and back, and the model you picked for each provider.
- The AI on this computer no longer stays on the processor after one graphics-card failure. It tries the card again (it gives up only after three failures in a row, and tries again a day later), and Settings has "Try the card again".
