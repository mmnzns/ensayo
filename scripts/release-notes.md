**First install**

- **Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit):** run `Ensayo_<version>_x64-setup.exe`. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Only once.
- **Mac, Apple silicon:** open `Ensayo_<version>_aarch64.dmg`, drag Ensayo to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open once.
- **Mac, Intel:** not available (Ensayo needs an Apple silicon Mac).

Already installed? Ensayo updates itself: it says when a new version is ready, and Settings, About has Check for updates. The `latest.json`, `.sig` and `.tar.gz` files are for that.
