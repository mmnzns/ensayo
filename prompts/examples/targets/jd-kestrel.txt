Senior Lifecycle Marketing Manager
Kestrel Pay · Toronto or remote · Full time

Kestrel Pay is a payments app that helps people send money to family abroad. We are a founder-led team of 40.

About the role
You will be our first lifecycle hire, and there is no marketing ops team behind you. You will build and run lifecycle from the first signup to the tenth payment, and report activation and early retention to the founders every week.

What you'll do
- Own onboarding, activation and early retention journeys across email, push and in-app
- Find where new customers drop off and fix it with product and design
- Run experiments and report what moved every week

What you bring
- 5+ years in lifecycle or CRM marketing at a consumer app
- Hands-on with a customer engagement platform and SQL
- You are happy to build it and run it yourself
