Marketing Operations Manager
Ledgerline · Remote (Canada) · Full time

Ledgerline makes close-and-reconcile software for finance teams at mid-size companies. We are a Series B company of about 300 people, and our customers are controllers and CFOs.

About the role
Marketing and sales share one pipeline, and right now neither team trusts the numbers. You will own the marketing stack end to end: the CRM, the marketing automation platform and the routing rules that get leads to the right rep. You will report marketing's share of pipeline to leadership every month.

What you'll do
- Keep lead data clean and reliable from form to opportunity
- Build and maintain lead scoring and routing with the sales operations team
- Own the marketing automation platform, its integrations and its data hygiene
- Build the monthly pipeline report for the leadership team
- Work with demand generation on campaign setup and tracking

What you bring
- 6+ years in marketing operations, ideally at a B2B software company
- Hands-on with a CRM and a marketing automation platform; SQL is a plus
- You have fixed broken handoffs between systems before
